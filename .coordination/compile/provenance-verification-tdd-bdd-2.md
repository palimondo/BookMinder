## Notes on non-FAITHFUL rows

**d006-R3 (T-29) — UNDERREACH.** The entry's rule is "do not assert that a methodology improved anything until at least one complete cycle of it has been executed and observed", and the transcript supports it exactly: at day-006:L1793-L1797 the author dismantles "44% code reduction", "0%→85% coverage" and the cost claim, ending on "we didn't even get to the refactoring stage of the TDD's inner loop with unit tests". T-29 as compiled is about executing a change before calling it ready — a different obligation, in a different tense. Nothing in T-29 (or in T-09, T-65, T-98) forbids claiming an effect for the methodology itself, and nothing captures the episode's second half: the comparison was between incomparable stages (a feature-complete predecessor vs a deliberate walking skeleton), which is why the numbers were meaningless rather than merely unverified.

**d005-R12 (T-45) — UNDERREACH.** The author's question was two-part: "are the repeated formatting failures … indication of some systematic issue in our setup? Also, could be exclude the specs from the type hint requirements?" (day-005:L1554-L1555).
