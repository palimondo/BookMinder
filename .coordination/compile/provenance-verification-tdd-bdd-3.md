## Roll-ups

### T-53 — split the fixture-coupling rule from the FIXME mechanism

T-53 currently reads: *Never weaken an assertion to make it pass against the fixture you happen to have, and never repair an unconditionally-true assertion by hardcoding a fixture-derived number — when the only available assertion would couple to incidental fixture shape, mark FIXME naming the right technique and owning layer, and move on.* The first two clauses are the durable part and are enforced twice in the window (day-020:L2566 blocked, L2573 blocked). The trailing clause should be cut or rewritten, because the episode ends without a FIXME and with the author retracting the technique he had named.

Proposed wording: *Never weaken an assertion to make it pass against the fixture you happen to have, and never repair an unconditionally-true assertion by hardcoding a fixture-derived number. When the property you want to pin is not this layer's to pin, say which layer owns it and leave the spec asserting only what this layer can falsify — the limit belongs to whichever spec controls the volume, not to a count copied out of the fixture.* That is what day-020:L2605 actually decided, and it removes the invitation to leave a FIXME naming a technique that may be wrong.

### T-54 and T-59 — point the fix-the-fixture clauses at T-76

Both rules send the reader to the fixture and stop. Two independent sessions show the author interrupting at exactly that step (day-015:L2200, day-018:L1308), in both cases because the agent was about to hand-author fixture data. Minimal change: in T-54 replace "fix the fixture to contain it" with "fix the fixture to contain it, by copying the real record (T-76) rather than authoring one", and in T-59 replace "fix the fixture first" with "fix the fixture first, by copying from the real source (T-76)". No new rule is needed; the cross-reference is the whole fix.

### T-56 — carry the author's emphasized clause

Proposed wording: *Before rewriting or replacing an existing spec, recover why it exists — `git log -S` the construct and read the change that introduced it — and enumerate the properties it constrains, then show the replacement constrains each one; if it does not, say so instead of proposing the edit.* Grounded at day-020:L56044-L56046 (the instruction) and L56057-L56085 (the agent carrying it out). This is the same obligation T-23 states for declarations; stating it here is what makes it reachable from the page a spec rewrite sends you to.
